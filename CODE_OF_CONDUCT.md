# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project welcoming, respectful, and free from harassment for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Respect differing viewpoints and lived experiences.
- Give and accept feedback in good faith.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and sharing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to `security@peerbits.com`. Reports will be handled privately and reviewed promptly.
